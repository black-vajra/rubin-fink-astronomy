Coverage and interpretation

  • Selected public-API samples, not the complete Rubin survey. A full-sized response means possible omissions. Even overlapping samples cannot prove complete coverage.
  • Newly downloaded is not newly observed. Historical observations remain labelled historical.
  • Photometry uses signed difference-image flux in nJy, separated by filter. It is not total brightness. Thresholds are exploratory and uncorrected for selection/multiple tests.
  • Position fits are within one broker-associated object, within one filter, on short arcs. They can reflect mis-association, centroid errors, parallax, proper motion, or artifacts. No cross-object linking or orbit fitting is performed; this is not a Planet Nine search.
  • Broker catalog matches are recorded when present. No independent catalog search, image-cutout inspection, or scientific confirmation is performed automatically.
  • Quality flags may be absent or unpopulated. Unknown quality is retained, but those measurements are excluded from the watchlist. Reliability is displayed, not trusted as a gate.
  • Daily reports use a rolling 24-hour schedule while this program runs. Stopping the computer stops collection; the next run reports the elapsed gap.
